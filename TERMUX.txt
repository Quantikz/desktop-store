Desktop Store on Termux (this phone is the shop)
================================================

Paste this one line in Termux. It downloads the shop and starts it.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Quantikz/desktop-store/main/termux-setup.sh | bash

Leave Termux open.

On THIS phone, open the browser:

  http://127.0.0.1:8080

Register the store (your name, username, password).

Other phones on the same Wi-Fi open the Wi-Fi address printed in Termux.

Next time, in Termux type:

  desktop-store

If Termux says “network is unreachable” or “failed to fetch packages-cf.termux.dev”:

  termux-change-repo

Pick a mirror group (try Default or Europe), then paste the install line again.
That is Termux not reaching its Python download site — not the shop itself.
